## WHAT FOCUSPHONE IS FOR

focusphone is a minimalist android launcher that helps you use your phone with intention:
a text-only home screen, profiles, app schedules, daily limits, a detox mode and a
conscious pause before opening an app.

**its locks are a personal commitment, not a security tool.** they are reinforced with
passwords and cooldowns so they cannot be undone on impulse, but they are designed so
nobody can be trapped:

| focusphone does not | because |
|---|---|
| act as a device administrator | it does not prevent its own uninstall |
| prevent switching launchers | you can always pick another home app in android settings |
| lock the system settings | android settings always stay reachable |
| survive clearing its data | clearing the app's storage removes every rule |
| supervise another person | it has no remote control, no reports and no admin mode |

therefore focusphone **must not be relied on** as parental control, as an employer's
device management tool, as a way to restrict someone else's phone, or as a guarantee that
an app cannot be opened. it is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any
condition; if your phone use worries you, consider talking to a professional.

focusphone is **beta software** (see [`readme.md`](../readme.md#project-status)). it stores
everything on the device and excludes itself from backups and device-to-device transfer
([`privacy.md`](./privacy.md)), so uninstalling it, clearing its data or changing phones
loses its configuration.
