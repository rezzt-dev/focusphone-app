focusphone privacy policy

last updated: 2026-05-13

1. information we collect

focusphone is an offline minimalist launcher. we do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

the app reads the list of installed applications solely to display them in the app drawer and favourite selector. this information never leaves your device.

2. permissions

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES — required to list installed apps for the launcher functionality.

3. data storage

all data (favourite apps, themes, settings, detox passwords) is stored locally on your device using android room and sharedpreferences. detox passwords are hashed using sha-256 before storage.

4. third-party services

focusphone does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries.

5. changes

we may update this policy as the app evolves. continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

6. contact

for questions about this policy, please contact the developer through the play store listing.
